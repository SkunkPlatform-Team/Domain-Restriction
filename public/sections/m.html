Domain Restriction

Restrict and validate authorized domains for your scripts

What is this?

Domain Restriction is a lightweight client-side system that allows you to authorize or block domains where your JavaScript can run.

Features

  • Authorized domains
  • Banned domains
  • Optional URL parameter validation
  • Password generation utilities
  • Obfuscated & tamper-resistant
